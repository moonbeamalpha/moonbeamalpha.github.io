Exam IQ

How Exam IQ predicts your score

Exam IQ is Azure Mastery's on-device engine that predicts your likely certification exam score as you practise. Here is what it actually does, and does not do.

What it is

What Exam IQ is

Exam IQ is Azure Mastery's on-device engine that predicts your likely score on a Microsoft certification exam as you practise, and recommends what to study next. It updates after every question you answer, rather than only at the end of a practice test.

The same on-device models also track a per-domain estimate of your ability, so you can see which exam domain is dragging your predicted score down, and knowledge-decay detection, which flags topics where retention has faded since you last reviewed them, based on the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve.

The model

The model: Item Response Theory and Bayesian updating

Score prediction is built on Item Response Theory, a psychometric model that scores each answer by the question's difficulty, discrimination, and guessing, rather than simply counting right and wrong answers. Bayesian updating then revises your ability estimate after every answer, so the predicted score moves as you practise instead of only recalculating at the end of a session.

Confidence range

What the confidence range means

Exam IQ reports a predicted score together with a confidence range — a low and a high estimate either side of the predicted number — rather than a single figure presented as certain. A tighter range reflects a more confident estimate, typically after more questions answered; a wider range means there is not yet enough evidence to narrow it further.

This is shown alongside, not instead of, an estimate of how likely you are to clear the pass mark, so you can see both where your score currently sits and how confident that estimate is.

Why on-device

Why it runs on-device

Exam IQ runs as a CoreML model bundled with the app, with a weighted-formula fallback if the model is unavailable — either way, the prediction happens on your device. Azure Mastery has no study-data server: no server, no cloud, and no study data ever leaves your iPhone.

Limits

What it cannot do

Exam IQ predicts your readiness from how you have practised in the app; it is not a guarantee that you will pass, and it cannot know the content, format, or scoring of the live exam, because none of that is available to Azure Mastery or any third party. Treat the predicted score as a study signal, not a promise.

Frequently asked

FAQs

What is Exam IQ?

Exam IQ is Azure Mastery's on-device engine that predicts your likely score on a Microsoft certification exam as you practise, updates after every question, and recommends what to study next based on where your predicted score is weakest.

Does Exam IQ need an internet connection?

No. Exam IQ runs as a CoreML model on your device, with a fallback formula if the model is unavailable. There is no server involved and no study data ever leaves your iPhone.

What does the confidence range mean?

Exam IQ reports a low and a high estimate either side of your predicted score, not a single number presented as certain. A tighter range means the estimate is more confident, typically after more questions answered; a wider range means there is not yet enough evidence to narrow it further.

Can Exam IQ guarantee I'll pass the exam?

No. Exam IQ predicts your readiness from how you have practised in the app. It is a study signal, not a guarantee — how you perform on exam day also depends on factors the app cannot see.

Does Exam IQ know what's on the real exam?

No. Exam IQ has no access to Microsoft's live exam content, format, or scoring — none of that is available to Azure Mastery or any third party. Its prediction is based entirely on your answers to Azure Mastery's own practice questions.
