AB-620 vs AB-410: agent builder or intelligent-app builder?

Both are new-in-2026 Copilot-adjacent Associate exams. See how they split the work, then pick the one that matches what you actually build.

Updated 4 September 2026 · ~9 min read

Quick answer

AB-620 or AB-410: the short version

Both exams sit inside the same Copilot Studio ecosystem, but they certify two different jobs. Which one you sit first should follow the work in front of you, not a ranking of which sounds more advanced.

Choose AB-620 if you build the agent itself

  • Right if your job is designing and extending custom agents inside Copilot Studio: topics, generative answers, knowledge grounding, MCP tools, multi-agent orchestration.
  • You're the person who decides how the agent reasons and responds.

Choose AB-410 if you build the application around it

  • Right if your job is building the Power Platform application, data model, and automation that consumes agents: Dataverse, model-driven and canvas apps, cloud flows, AI Hub prompts.
  • You're the person who decides how the business actually uses what the agent produces.

Neither is a formal prerequisite for the other. See "Do you need one before the other?" below for how the two certifications fit together when you own the whole solution.

What AB-620 actually tests

What AB-620 actually tests

AB-620 is scenario-driven and Copilot Studio-specific. Expect a configuration screen or a described business constraint, and a question about what to change or which component to use.

AB-620 skills measured (outline effective 21 April 2026)
DomainWeight
Plan & configure agent solutions30–35%
Integrate & extend agents40–45%
Test & manage agents20–25%
  • Planning covers integration with enterprise systems, identity strategy, channel and deployment planning, and responsible AI, then agent flows and configuring topics: trigger phrases, tools, adaptive cards, and variables.
  • Integrate and extend, the largest domain, covers connecting to enterprise knowledge sources such as Azure AI Search and Power Platform connectors, and adding tools including MCP and REST APIs.
  • The section most candidates underestimate is multi-agent collaboration: wiring a Foundry agent or a Fabric data agent into an orchestrator, and cross-agent solutions with the Agent2Agent protocol.
  • Test and manage covers evaluation methods and application lifecycle management — solutions, environment variables, and Power Platform Pipelines rather than manual copy-paste between environments.

What AB-410 actually tests

What AB-410 actually tests

AB-410 is task-based and spans two different app-building paradigms plus a heavyweight automation domain, so it rewards candidates who are comfortable moving between Dataverse, both app types, and Power Fx in the same session.

AB-410 skills measured (outline effective 15 May 2026)
DomainWeight
Create a foundation for intelligent applications25–30%
Create intelligent applications25–30%
Build business application logic & automation40–45%
  • The foundation domain covers Dataverse table and column design, relationships, prompt columns, security, and choosing the right AI-enabled build approach and environment type before you start.
  • Create intelligent applications splits between model-driven apps — forms, views, generative pages built from natural language, dashboards — and canvas apps, where accessibility, reusable components, and error handling matter as much as layout.
  • Business logic and automation, the largest domain, is where AB-410 differs most from a plain app-building exam: cloud flow triggers and connectors, AI Hub prompts consumed from both apps and flows.
  • It also tests the distinctions between business rules, business process flows, and calculated, rollup, or formula columns that recur throughout the exam.

Prerequisite question

Do you need one before the other?

No. AB-620 and AB-410 are both standalone Associate certifications, and Microsoft does not require either one as a prerequisite for the other.

  • Sit AB-620 without ever touching AB-410's Dataverse and app-building content, or sit AB-410 without ever building an agent from scratch in Copilot Studio.
  • Where they genuinely connect is in what they build together: AB-620 certifies the agent's internal logic, AB-410 certifies the application, data model, and automation around it.
  • A single Copilot solution — say, a support assistant with a Dataverse-backed case log, a canvas app for reviewing flagged conversations, and an approval flow for escalations — draws on both skill sets at once.
  • Own that kind of end-to-end solution? Sitting both maps directly onto your job. Own only one half? Sit the one that matches it and treat the other as optional.
  • Deciding purely on order: sit whichever reflects your stronger existing skill first — daily Copilot Studio builders find AB-620 faster, Dataverse-and-canvas-app builders find AB-410 faster — since the second exam's shared Power Platform foundation then needs less relearning.

Career value

How each certification helps your career

Each certification signals a different part of the Copilot solution to a hiring manager.

  • AB-620 is the credential to point to when a role description says "Copilot Studio developer", "AI agent builder", or asks specifically for agent orchestration, MCP tooling, or multi-agent design.
  • It signals you can be trusted with the parts of a Copilot solution that are hardest to get wrong: groundedness, tool access, and how agents hand off to one another.
  • AB-410 is the credential for "Power Platform developer" or "low-code application builder" roles that now expect AI literacy as standard, not a bonus skill.
  • It's also the direct successor path for anyone who previously built toward PL-200 — the PL-200T00 course was replaced by AB-410T00, and prior PL-200 experience with Dataverse and canvas apps transfers cleanly.
  • Neither guarantees a role or pay rise; both give a structured way to demonstrate an in-demand skill set, backed by a Microsoft-set bar rather than a self-assessment.

Take both?

Should you take both AB-620 and AB-410?

For anyone who owns a Copilot solution end to end, taking both is the strongest option on the table.

  • Microsoft's own certification pathing groups AB-620 and AB-410 together for exactly this reason: one exam proves you can build the agent, the other proves you can build everything it depends on and everything that depends on it.
  • Only touch one side of that boundary? Taking both is optional — a pure Copilot Studio developer gets little extra signal from AB-410's Dataverse and canvas-app content, and a pure app builder who never designs agent logic gets little extra signal from AB-620's orchestration-heavy domains.
  • Sit the one that matches the job and revisit the other only if the role expands.
  • Plan to sit both? Study them in sequence rather than in parallel so the shared Power Platform foundation works in your favour instead of doubling your workload. The AB-620 study guide lays out a four-week plan you can use as the first half of a combined project.

Frequently asked

AB-620 vs AB-410 FAQs

Do I need AB-620 before AB-410, or the other way round?

Neither. Both are standalone Associate exams with no formal prerequisite between them, and Microsoft's booking system doesn't check which one you hold before letting you schedule either. Take them in whichever order matches the work in front of you.

What's the real difference between AB-620 and AB-410?

AB-620 tests designing and extending the agent: topics, orchestration, knowledge sources, tools, multi-agent protocols. AB-410 tests building the Power Platform application around it: Dataverse, model-driven and canvas apps, cloud flows, AI Hub prompts. AB-410 stays low-code throughout; AB-620 leans more code-first, since MCP and Agent2Agent involve structured configuration Copilot Studio's canvas doesn't fully abstract.

Is AB-410 the same as the old PL-200?

No, but it's the successor path for makers who previously targeted PL-200. The PL-200T00 course was replaced by AB-410T00, and AB-410 folds in the AI Hub, generative-page, and Copilot-integration content PL-200 never covered. Prior Dataverse and Power Apps experience is a genuine head start, but doesn't cover the AI Hub objectives alone.

Which is harder, AB-620 or AB-410?

Comparably demanding, but hard differently. AB-620 concentrates difficulty in Integrate & Extend (40-45%), where multi-agent orchestration and Agent2Agent require precise distinctions. AB-410 spreads difficulty across two app types plus a 40-45% automation domain, rewarding breadth over depth in any single area.

Do AB-620 and AB-410 expire the same way?

Yes. Both are Associate certifications, renewed annually through a free, unproctored assessment on Microsoft Learn during the six-month window before expiry. Passing one doesn't affect the other's renewal clock; each renews independently.

Can I take AB-620 and AB-410 close together?

Yes. There's no cooling-off period between different exam codes, only between repeat attempts at the same one. Since both assume familiarity with the Power Platform environment model and ALM basics, studying them back to back means the second exam's foundation section moves faster than the first did.
