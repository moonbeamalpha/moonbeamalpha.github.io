What it is

What Azure Mastery is

Azure Mastery is a native SwiftUI exam-prep app for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, built for Microsoft certification exams across Azure, AI, Data, Security, Power Platform, and GitHub. It runs on iOS 18 and iPadOS 18 or later, with an Apple Watch companion for streaks and daily progress.

Its core features: Exam IQ, an on-device engine that predicts your likely exam score with a confidence range as you practise; an adaptive study plan that reorders what to revise next based on your answers; Answer Coach, which turns a missed answer into a private explanation grounded in the app's own authored rationales; and knowledge-decay tracking that flags topics fading from memory before they cost you marks on exam day.

The app is free to download, with optional one-time in-app purchases unlocking full per-exam question banks. There is no subscription for core study features, and no account is required. Core study — the question bank, Exam IQ, the adaptive study plan, and Answer Coach's grounded explanations — works entirely offline, with no study-data server and no behavioural telemetry.

Why it exists

Why Azure Mastery exists

Most exam-prep apps optimise for volume — thousands of questions, each with little more than a single correct answer marked. Azure Mastery is built around a different premise: a candidate learns faster from understanding why each option is right or wrong than from memorising an answer key.

Every question in the app carries an explanation, and where an option-by-option rationale is written, it covers the wrong answers as well as the right one — so getting a question wrong becomes a study session, not just a red mark.

How it's built

How it's built

Every practice question is written against Microsoft's own published skills outline for that exam, checked by an automated gate on every change, and watched afterwards for stale content — see how we write and verify questions.
